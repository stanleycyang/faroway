# Competitor Analysis — Faroway Content Squad

## Target Competitors

### 1. Wanderlog (wanderlog.com)
**Traffic:** ~2M monthly organic visits
**Content model:** Massive destination-guide library + trip planning templates

**Top content patterns:**
- "Best things to do in [City]" — very high volume, editorial style
- "X-day [City] itinerary" — converts trip planners
- "[City] travel guide" — broad authority articles
- Packing lists by destination/season

**Keyword opportunities for Faroway:**
- Anything AI-enhanced: "AI-generated [city] itinerary" — Faroway owns this angle
- Comparison: "Wanderlog vs AI travel planner" — commercial intent, converts
- Template + AI: "Free [city] itinerary template" → upsell Faroway's AI
- Mobile-first itinerary tools

---

### 2. Layla.ai (layla.ai)
**Traffic:** ~500K monthly organic visits
**Content model:** AI-forward, comparison articles, "best of" lists

**Top content patterns:**
- "Best AI trip planners of [year]"
- "AI travel planner vs [tool]" comparisons
- Destination inspirations

**Keyword opportunities for Faroway:**
- "Layla AI travel planner alternative" — high commercial intent
- "Best AI travel planner 2026" — we can out-rank with more depth
- "Free AI itinerary generator" — volume play
- "AI travel planner that books" — future feature angle

---

### 3. Roadtrippers (roadtrippers.com/magazine)
**Traffic:** ~800K monthly organic visits
**Content model:** Road trip focused editorial magazine

**Top content patterns:**
- Road trip route guides (Route 66, Pacific Coast Highway, etc.)
- "Best road trips in [State/Country]"
- Quirky/hidden gem destinations

**Keyword opportunities for Faroway:**
- "AI road trip planner" — growing search term
- "Road trip itinerary generator" — tool intent
- "Best road trip routes [year]" — seasonal traffic

---

### 4. TripAdvisor (tripadvisor.com)
**Traffic:** Hundreds of millions/month — don't fight head-on
**Content model:** UGC reviews + editorial guides

**Keyword opportunities for Faroway:**
- Long-tail "hidden gems" and "off the beaten path" — less competitive
- "AI vs TripAdvisor" comparisons — commercial intent
- "Better than TripAdvisor" — people actively searching alternatives

---

### 5. Nomad List (nomadlist.com)
**Traffic:** ~1M monthly organic visits
**Content model:** Digital nomad city rankings + community

**Top content patterns:**
- "Best cities for digital nomads in [year]"
- Cost of living guides
- Remote work travel tips

**Keyword opportunities for Faroway:**
- "AI travel planner for digital nomads"
- "Plan a workation with AI"
- "Remote work travel itinerary"

---

### 6. Lonely Planet (lonelyplanet.com)
**Traffic:** ~10M monthly — compete on long-tail only
**Content model:** Authoritative destination guides

**Keyword opportunities for Faroway:**
- AI angle on everything: "AI-powered [destination] guide"
- "Better than guidebooks" angle
- Specific niche destinations LP covers poorly

---

## Skyscraper Principles for Faroway

1. **Always lead with the AI angle** — Wanderlog/TripAdvisor can't claim this; Faroway can
2. **Be more specific** — "3-day Paris itinerary for first-timers on $150/day" beats "Paris travel guide"
3. **Include real logistics** — actual flight routes, neighborhood breakdowns, timing, costs
4. **Add interactivity hook** — end every article with "Generate your personalized [destination] itinerary with Faroway"
5. **Cluster internally** — link every destination guide → relevant itinerary template → AI travel tips
6. **Update signals** — include "Last updated [month year]" to signal freshness
